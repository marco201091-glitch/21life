21Life helps Commander playgroups track games without spreadsheets.

Features:

- Real-time life, commander damage, infect, eliminations, and match finalization
- Playgroup history with players, decks, commanders, rankings, and awards
- Deck import from Archidekt and Moxfield
- Optional public playgroup pages for rankings and color trends
- Offline recovery for active live games
- Italian and English interface

This app is unofficial fan content. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Scryfall, EDHREC, Archidekt, Moxfield, or other mentioned services.

The app depends on hosted network services for email/password authentication, account sync, playgroup history, deck import, card imagery, and anti-abuse protection. The F-Droid version excludes Google sign-in, push notifications, and Sentry diagnostics.
